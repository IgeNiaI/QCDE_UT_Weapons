Bio Rifle (deathmatch)

Sprites/Weapons/BioRifle/: U1, UT2004, UC1, UC2, UT99 and Modded (ID 20).
Prefixes U1/U4/U5/U6/U9/MD; fourth sprite character E.
Prepare: A set A-Z, 1 tic each, firing allowed; current artwork static.
Idle: C set A-F, 1 tic each, static artwork.
Primary/recovery: E set A-C, original ten-tic animation and weapon offsets.
Charge/alternate/recovery: I set A-V; all ten original charge stages and
their no-ammo/prediction hold states are preserved. A-C are recovery frames.
Brightmap sets B/D/F/J; 57 independent Modded frames/maps, UT2004 base.
Original G sprites/brightmaps remain as compatibility and pickup sources.

Sounds/Weapons/BioRifle/Modded/: fire, Quad fire, charge and eight impacts.
Prepare/idle are silent placeholders with commented SNDINFO mappings.
Screen flash: WeaponSkin_BioRifle_ScreenBlend_Modded in LANGUAGE.UTD.
Menu preview: Graphics/Menu/SkinPreviews/BioRifle/SKINGEMD.png.

MDZE A-F: projectile, sticky glob and impact sprites in Modded/_Z_projectile/.
Models and six animated skins: MD3/PROJECTILES/Modded/BioRifle/.
Decorate/Weapons/Modded/BioRifle.QCD contains independent copies of all ten
BioBall1DM_Modded through BioBall10DM_Modded projectiles, three
BioBallSticky1DM_Modded through BioBallSticky3DM_Modded variants, and
BioBallDeathFX_Modded (currently using core DB63 artwork).
These retain all gameplay properties, direct/splash damage, sticky lifetime,
hit feedback, debris counts, client-prediction workarounds and unlag waits.
The original actors, Unreal variants, Ripper and world pickups are untouched.

Generation manifests: Tools/WeaponSkinAssets/BioRifle.json and
Tools/WeaponSkinAssets/BioRifleActors.json. Run the corresponding
Tools/WeaponSkinAssets.cjs and Tools/WeaponSkinActors.cjs generators.
Generators refuse to overwrite customized output.
