Sprite prefixes: U4 for UT2004; U6 for Unreal Championship 2; MD for Modded.
The fourth sprite-name character is 4 (Link Gun).
Skin folders: UT2004 and UC2.

Prepare: 26 frames, 1 tic each; firing is allowed. Current artwork is static.
Idle: 10 animated frames, 4 tics each; A_WeaponReady runs every tic.
The original 40-tic idle loop is preserved.
Fire: A is resting; B-H and I-O are alternating shots, 1 tic per frame.
Each shot lasts 7 tics; B-C and I-J are bright muzzle-flash frames.
The one-tic fire-end state uses the resting frame and still allows firing.
Repeated firing frames reuse idle artwork through TEXTURES.UTD.
UT2004 brightmaps follow the corresponding original frames; UC2 has none.

Projectile behavior, damage, ammo use, sounds, recoil and firing offsets are
unchanged, including the separate deathmatch projectile variants.

Modded skin (ID 20)

Modded assets are in the Modded subfolders above, using UT2004 as the base.
Each weapon frame has its own image and brightmap, including repeated frames.
The animated idle loop and both seven-tic firing sequences are unchanged.
Prepare and idle sounds are silent placeholders; their SNDINFO mappings are
commented out until replaced. Fire, Quad fire and all seven impact sounds
have separate Modded assets. The screen flash color is in LANGUAGE.UTD.

Other Modded frames

Projectile sprites: Sprites/Weapons/LinkGun/Modded/_Z_projectile/
UTPulseWeak_Modded and UTPulseWeakDM_Modded inherit their respective gameplay
properties and hit feedback. The deathmatch projectile keeps 25 damage and
its original collision flags and decal. Impact timing, lights and the extra
obituary tic are unchanged.
