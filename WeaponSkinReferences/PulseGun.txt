Pulse Gun

Sprites/Weapons/PulseGun/: UT2003, UC1, UT99 and Modded (ID 20).
Prepare: A set, A-Z, 1 tic each; firing is allowed.
Idle: C set, A-F, 1 tic each; current artwork is static.
Primary fire: E set, A-N; alternating five-tic shots and original refire.
Alternate fire: I set, A-R; original two-tic beam pairs and one-tic fire end.
Matching brightmap sets: B, D, F and J.
Sprite prefixes: U3, U5, U9 and MD. Fourth character: D.
Legacy G sprites and brightmaps remain as compatibility sources and pickups.
Existing skins use aliases for repeated frames. Modded has 64 independent
weapon images and brightmaps, based on UT2003.

Sounds: Sounds/Weapons/PulseGun/Modded/.
Prepare/idle sounds are silent placeholders with commented SNDINFO mappings.
Primary loop/end, alternate loop, Quad loops and seven impacts are separate.
Original sound volumes (0.60 primary, 0.80 alternate) are preserved.
Menu preview: Graphics/Menu/SkinPreviews/PulseGun/SKINGDMD.png.
Screen flash color: WeaponSkin_PulseGun_ScreenBlend_Modded in LANGUAGE.UTD.

MDZD A-O: projectile, normal impact and Lava explosion in Modded/_Z_projectile/.
UTPulseStrong_Modded and UTPulseStrongDM_Modded preserve their original
damage, flags, hit feedback, Lava upgrade and explosion timings.
PulseBeamPuff_Modded, PulseBeamPuffDM_Modded, PulseBeamPuffNoSound_Modded,
PGworldactor_Modded and PGworldactor2_Modded are separate beam/effect hooks;
these retain original artwork until their inherited states are replaced.
Seeker piercing, DM damage, invisible-beam option, ammo flags, lights,
recoil/flash toggles and UT99's original fire-end animation are unchanged.

Tools/WeaponSkinAssets/PulseGun.json describes the generated resource section.
Run node Tools/WeaponSkinAssets.cjs Tools/WeaponSkinAssets/PulseGun.json
from the repository root to verify/regenerate assets and resource definitions.
