Flak Cannon Mini

Assets

Weapon sprites and brightmaps: Sprites/Weapons/FlakCannonMini/
Sounds: Sounds/Weapons/FlakMini/
Menu previews: Graphics/Menu/SkinPreviews/FlakMini/

Weapon frames

Use                     Set    Frames    Brightmap set
Prepare                 A      A-Z       B
Idle                    C      A-F       D
Fire                    E      A-P       F

Sprite prefixes: U1 for Unreal 1 Flak; U2 for Unreal 1 Stinger; MD for Modded.
The fourth sprite-name character is 3 (Flak Mini).
Skin folders: U1_Flak and U1_Stinger.

Prepare: 26 frames, 1 tic each; firing is allowed.
Idle: 6 frames, 1 tic each. Current artwork is static.
Fire: A is resting; B-D run for 1 tic each; E-P run for 2 tics each.
The firing sequence remains 27 tics, with the original per-tic offsets.
Identical frames and brightmaps share images through TEXTURES.UTD and GLDEFS.UTD.
Stinger uses one shared brightmap for all frames.

World pickup frames U1G3 Y/Z and Stinger projectile sprites TRYD are unchanged.
Projectile behavior, damage, ammunition use, sounds and recoil are unchanged.

Modded skin (ID 20)

Modded assets are in the Modded subfolders above, using Unreal 1 Flak as the base.
Each weapon frame has its own image and brightmap, including repeated frames.
Prepare and idle sounds are silent placeholders; their SNDINFO mappings are
commented out until replaced. Fire, Quad fire and bounce sounds are separate.
The screen flash color is configurable in LANGUAGE.UTD.

UTFlakMiniShellDM_Modded and UTFlakMiniShellBouncedDM_Modded inherit the
original projectile properties and hit feedback, with separate trail hooks:
UTFlakMiniTrailFX_Modded and UTFlakMiniTrailFXBounced_Modded.
These currently reuse the core NAIL projectile and DB60 trail sprites.
The nine-projectile volley, ammo use, bounce behavior and extra obituary tic
are unchanged. World pickup frames are not affected by this skin.
