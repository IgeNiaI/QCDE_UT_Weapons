Weapon frames

Idle                    C      A-F       D
Fire                    E      A-I       F

Prepare: 26 frames, 1 tic each; firing is allowed.
Idle: 6 frames, 1 tic each. Current artwork is static.
Fire: A is resting; B-I alternate while firing, at all firing speeds.
Fire end: A is resting; B-P slow the barrels to a stop.
Unreal 1 uses B-P; other skins use B-K.
Identical frames and brightmaps share images.

Modded uses UT2004 as its base. Each frame has its own image and brightmap.
