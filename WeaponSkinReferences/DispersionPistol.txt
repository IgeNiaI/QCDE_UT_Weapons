Fire                    M      O                Q                A-L      N / P / R

Sprite prefixes: U1 for Unreal 1; U6 for Unreal Championship 2; MD for Modded.
The fourth sprite-name character is G (Dispersion Pistol).
Example: U1AG is base prepare; U1OG is single-upgrade fire.

Prepare: 26 frames, 1 tic each; firing is allowed.
Idle: 6 frames, 1 tic each. Current artwork is static.
Fire: A is resting; B-C are the first firing image; D-E are the second;
F-L recover using the resting image. B-L run for 11 tics total.
Either upgrade uses the single-upgrade sets; both use double-upgrade sets.
Identical frames and brightmaps share images through TEXTURES.UTD and GLDEFS.UTD.
U1 has one shared brightmap per upgrade level; UC2 has no brightmaps.

World pickup frames U1GG Y/Z are unchanged and are not idle animation frames.
Projectiles, damage, sounds, recoil and firing offsets are unchanged.

Modded skin (ID 20)

Modded assets are in the Modded subfolders above, using Unreal 1 as the base.
Each weapon frame has its own image and brightmap, including repeated frames.
Folder suffixes: no suffix for base, one_upgrade for either upgrade,
two_upgrades for both upgrades.
Prepare and idle sounds are silent placeholders; their SNDINFO mappings are
commented out until replaced. Fire, Quad fire and impact sounds are separate.
Screen flash colors are configurable per upgrade level in LANGUAGE.UTD.

Other Modded frames

MDVG A-B                Base projectile (all eight viewing angles)
MDWG A-B                One-upgrade projectile (all eight viewing angles)
MDXG A-B                Two-upgrade projectile (all eight viewing angles)

Projectile folders: _V_projectile, _W_projectile_one_upgrade,
_X_projectile_two_upgrades.
UTDispr1_Modded, UTDispr3_Modded and UTDispr5_Modded inherit gameplay and
hit feedback from the corresponding original projectiles. Their Death states
are separate replacement hooks; they currently reuse the core LBA2 A-H impact
sprites and the original dynamic lights, with a Modded impact sound.
