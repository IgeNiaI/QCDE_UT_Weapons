Sprite prefixes: U4 for UT2004; U5 for UC1; U6 for UC2; U9 for UT99; MD for Modded.
The fourth sprite-name character is C (Flak Cannon).
Skin folders: UT2004, UC1, UC2 and UT99.

Prepare: 26 frames, 1 tic each; firing is allowed.
Idle: 6 frames, 1 tic each. Current artwork is static.
Primary fire: A is resting; B-V preserve the original 27-tic sequence.
Alternate fire: A is resting; B-R reuse original F-V, preserving 33 tics.
Both firing modes retain their original per-tic weapon offsets.
Identical frames share images through TEXTURES.UTD.
UT99 brightmaps follow the original frames; UC1 uses one shared brightmap.
UT2004 and UC2 have no brightmaps.

World pickup frames U4GC Y/Z and original grenade sprites FLAK are unchanged.
Gameplay properties, upgrades, ammo use, sounds and recoil are unchanged.

Modded skin (ID 20)

Modded assets are in the Modded subfolders above, using UT2004 as the base.
All 72 weapon frames have independent images and black placeholder brightmaps.
Prepare and idle sounds are silent placeholders; their SNDINFO mappings are
commented out until replaced. Primary/alternate fire, their Quad variants,
explosion and three bounce sounds have separate assets.
The screen flash color is configurable in LANGUAGE.UTD.

MDZC A1-A8: directional grenade sprites in Modded/_Z_grenade/.
Grenade model and skin: MD3/PROJECTILES/Modded/FlakCannon/.
MODELDEF.WPN maps both normal and deathmatch Modded grenades independently.

Projectile/effect replacement hooks in Decorate/Weapons/SShotgun.QCD:
UTFlakShell_Modded and UTFlakShellDM_Modded;
UTFlakShellBounced_Modded, UTFlakShellBouncedUpgraded_Modded,
and UTFlakShellBouncedDM_Modded;
UTFlakBomb_Modded and UTFlakBombDM_Modded;
UTFlakShellTrailFX_Modded, UTFlakShellTrailFXBounced_Modded,
and UTBombTrailFX_Modded;
UTFlakExploDebris_Modded, UTFlakExploDebrisDM_Modded,
UTFlakExploParticle_Modded, UTFlakExplosion_Modded,
and UTFlakExplosionDM_Modded.

Fragment/trail/explosion hooks currently retain core artwork (including
NAIL, DB60 and X037). Replace their states to use custom effect sprites.
The normal primary volley fires 9 fragments, or 17 with the Flame upgrade;
deathmatch always fires 9 with its original deterministic spread.
Normal grenades spawn 10 debris pieces, or 19 with Flame; Speed makes debris
safe for its owner. Deathmatch retains 10 debris pieces and its own damage.
Hit feedback, obituary tics, bounce behavior and the normal 99-tic versus
deathmatch 10-tic explosion lifetime are preserved.
